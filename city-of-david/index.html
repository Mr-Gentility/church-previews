Who We Are

Raising people who love God and transform their world

City of David Church is a vibrant, Bible-believing family in the heart of Lagos. We exist to help every person encounter Jesus, grow in faith and discover their God-given purpose.

Whether you are new to faith or have walked with God for years, there is a seat saved for you.

  • Spirit-filled worship
  • Sound Bible teaching
  • Thriving youth & children
  • Community outreach
